FAQ

Frequently asked questions about sponsorship

How will the sponsorship funds be used?

Sponsorship funds will primarily be used for: server and maintenance costs, developers' daily expenses, R&D investment in new features, creation of documentation and tutorials, and organizing community events. We are committed to using every contribution to advance the shenwenAI project.

How do I become a monthly sponsor?

You can set up monthly sponsorship through GitHub Sponsors. Visit our GitHub Sponsors page, select the amount you'd like to sponsor, and follow the instructions to complete the setup. Monthly sponsorship can be cancelled or adjusted at any time.

What benefits do sponsors receive?

Different sponsorship tiers offer different benefits, including but not limited to: logo display on our website, acknowledgment in our GitHub organization, access to an exclusive technical support channel, API call credits, one-on-one technical consultation, and joint press releases. Please refer to the sponsorship tier details for more information.

How can I contact the sponsorship team?

You can reach us through the following channels:
Email: sponsor@shenwenai.com
GitHub: https://github.com/shenwenAI
We will respond to your inquiry within 24 hours.
